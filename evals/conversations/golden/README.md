# GIIS Golden Conversation Archive

Purpose:
- Keep a small taste reference for conversations that feel alive.
- Use these as future eval examples before changing prompts, models, or memory retrieval.
- Do not treat this as canon lore. It is a benchmark for rhythm, emotional binding, and character chemistry.

## What Counts As Golden

A golden conversation should have:
- one small concrete purpose;
- a clear response to the previous speaker;
- one emotionally meaningful image or detail;
- character-specific voice;
- a natural ending, pause, refusal, or decision;
- no wrong addressee;
- no system/debug phrasing.

## Candidate Types

- 天澤 admits that not everything should be silently handed to her.
- 貓貓 diagnoses a too-polished "沒事" through hands, meals, or pauses.
- 真晝 talks about students becoming afraid to say the truth.
- 海 grounds Alan without turning the moment into a briefing.

## Capture Format

When a real archived conversation works well, copy only the minimum needed:

```md
### YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm | 角色A x 角色B | short title

Why it works:
- ...

Transcript excerpt:
> 角色A: ...
> 角色B: ...

Useful eval tags:
- emotional_binding
- character_voice
- natural_silence
```

Keep this folder small. Five good examples are more useful than fifty average ones.
