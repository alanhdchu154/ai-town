# Underworld Media Review Gate

Use this before any Underworld media package is rendered, uploaded, or made
public.

## Verdict

`PASS`, `FIX_FIRST`, or `BLOCKED`.

## Checks

### 1. Evidence

- The claim points to a real Underworld report, log, transcript, paper, or
  experiment.
- Sample-pending states are labeled as sample-pending.
- Failures are presented honestly.

### 2. Claims

- No claim that AI society, friendship, jealousy, bullying, trust, lying, or
  leadership has emerged unless current evidence supports that exact wording.
- Prefer question framing when evidence is exploratory:
  "Can AI form groups?" rather than "AI formed groups."

### 3. Privacy

- No OAuth secrets, private keys, student/private data, or unreviewed personal
  records.
- Screenshots are scrubbed before use.

### 4. Runtime Safety

- The media pipeline did not mutate Convex state.
- The media pipeline did not restart, kick, or repair the world.
- The media pipeline did not change prompts, memory, souls, experienceLogs, or
  sleepNotes.

### 5. Copyright / Attribution

- Paper videos cite title, authors, year, and source link.
- Fork/source attribution is included where relevant.
- Generated visuals are labeled as illustrative unless they are real UI footage.

### 6. Upload Safety

- Upload package is prepared.
- No automatic publishing.
- Public release requires explicit Alan approval in the current conversation.
