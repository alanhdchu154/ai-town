# Seed Papers

## Park et al. 2023

Title:
`Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior`

Link:
`https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442`

Why it matters:
This is the AI Town / generative agents baseline for memory, reflection,
planning, and believable social behavior.

Underworld angle:
What AI Town gets right about memory, and where Underworld needs stronger
evidence for continuity and subjective residue.

## Russell 1980

Title:
`A Circumplex Model of Affect`

Link:
`https://pdodds.w3.uvm.edu/research/papers/others/1980/russell1980a.pdf`

Why it matters:
It gives a simple affect-space lens that is more useful than treating emotions
as independent labels.

Underworld angle:
Why flat emotion scores are not enough for believable characters.
