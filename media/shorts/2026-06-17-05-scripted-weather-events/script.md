# Script

My AI world had events,
but many of them were closer to weather.

They appeared in the background.
They changed the mood.
Characters could talk about them.

But often, nobody actually did anything because of them.

No one moved rooms.
No follow-up was scheduled.
No relationship changed in a durable way.

That is why v0.2 matters.

An event should not only be text in a prompt.

It should become a cause:
someone notices,
someone acts,
someone remembers,
and the next scene is different because the first one happened.
