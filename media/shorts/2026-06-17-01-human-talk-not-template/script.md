# Script

My AI characters sounded emotional, but not alive.

One character kept opening by diagnosing tiny body signals.
Another kept turning care into the same kind of gentle sentence.

The problem was not that the model was stupid.
The problem was that I had made the character archetype the opening move.

So every time Maomao talked, she diagnosed.
Every time Mahiru talked, she comforted.
Every time Umi talked, she organized.

Humans do not usually start from their archetype.
They start from what just happened, who is standing in front of them, and what mood they are already in.

So the fix was small but important:
personality is the how, not the what.

The first sentence has to grow from the situation.

That is the difference between a character who has a voice
and a character who is just performing a prompt.
