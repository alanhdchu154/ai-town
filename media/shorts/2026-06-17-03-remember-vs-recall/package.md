Core question:
What is the difference between storing a memory and being reminded of it?

Answer:
The text can still exist, but without a reachable cue or embedding handle, it
cannot be actively recalled. Forgetting in Underworld should eventually mean
sinking into deep dormancy, not deleting the soul.

Hook:
What would it mean for an AI character to forget?

Claim boundary:
