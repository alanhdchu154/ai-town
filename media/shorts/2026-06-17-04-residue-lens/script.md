# Script

The same event should not become the same memory for everyone.

If Umi sees someone skip lunch,
she may think about Alan's overload and world stability.

If Mahiru sees it,
she may wonder who is quietly not okay.

If Maomao sees it,
she may treat it as a symptom.

The event is objective.
The residue is subjective.

That is the part I want Underworld to learn next:
not just what happened,
but who carried it,
and what kind of person they became for a moment after carrying it.
