# Script

I realized I built my AI world backwards.

The characters had souls, memories, relationship notes, and a lot of prompt rules.

But I kept adding guards:
do not repeat this phrase,
do not sound too poetic,
do not over-explain,
do not mirror the other character.

That was useful scar tissue.
But it was also a symptom.

The real problem was that the situation was too thin.

If nothing actually happened in the world,
the model has to invent emotional movement from style alone.

So v0.2 is not "more prompts."

It is making events real:
something happens,
someone moves,
someone reacts,
and tomorrow has a reason to feel different.

Speech should come from life.
Not from a longer list of bans.
