Core question:
Why did Underworld need so many dialogue guards?

Answer:
Because the situation was thin. We were forcing the output not to collapse
instead of giving characters real events, relationships, and state to speak from.

Hook:
I realized I built my AI world backwards.

Claim boundary:
This is a design diagnosis. It does not claim v0.2 is implemented.
